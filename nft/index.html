    Inclusion method
    1. The collection has a publicly documented floor price above 0.5 ETH.
    2. Launch date, mint price, and supply are attributable to the project or to a publication, and are shown at the granularity the source supports — a month stays a month.
    3. Peak floor is stated only where a single figure is sourced. Where it is not, the page shows the sourced high-water event instead of a number.
    4. Live floors come from the CoinGecko NFT collections API and are never mixed with historical figures without a label.

    ⚠ Floor price is the cheapest listing, not a clearing price for the collection. Survivorship bias is severe: almost no NFT launch reaches these floors. Avoid leverage and predefine position size.
